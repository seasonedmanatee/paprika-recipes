Masala Pasta Recipe (Indian Style Pasta)

Servings: 2 · Prep: 15 minutes · Cook: 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pasta
  • 2 tablespoon olive oil or butter (divided) (adjust to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 3 cloves garlic chopped finely
  • ⅓ teaspoon salt (+ ½ tsp for boiling pasta)
  • ½ teaspoon red chili powder (adjust to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala or pav bhaji masala or any other masala powder
  • ½ teaspoon kasuri methi (or 2 tbsps coriander leaves)
  • 2 tablespoon cream as needed (optional)
  • 1 large onion chopped finely
  • ¾ to 1 cup tomatoes chopped mashed or pureed (do not use very sour tomatoes)
  • 2 carrots chopped or as needed
  • ¼ to ½ cup green peas
  • ¼ cup capsicum (bell pepper) – chopped

Directions

  1. In a large pot, bring 6 cups of water to a boil.
  2. When the water begins to boil rapidly, add half teaspoon salt and pasta.
  3. Cook till al dente on a medium to high flame.
  4. I suggest referring the instructions on the pack of pasta.
  5. While the pasta cooks, rinse and chop the veggies.
  6. Drain off the pasta to a colander when done and rinse in cold water.
  7. Set aside. Reserve 3 to 4 tablespoons of pasta cooked water.
  8. How to make masala pasta
  9. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a pan. When the oil turns hot, add cumin.
  10. When they begin to splutter, then add finely chopped garlic.
  11. Fry till it begins to smell good for a minute or two.
  12. Add onions and fry until they turn pink.
  13. Then add chopped veggies and fry for about 2 minutes.
  14. Next mash the tomatoes well and add it to the pan along with salt.
  15. Cook until the tomatoes turn soft and blend well with veggies. The raw smell of tomatoes should disappear.
  16. Add kasuri methi, red chili powder and masala powder.
  17. Stir well and fry for a minute. Switch off the stove. If using cream add now and stir.
  18. Add cooked pasta and 1 tablespoon oil. Toss well. Adjust salt as needed.
  19. Add coriander leaves. If the pasta looks dry, then pour 1 to 2 tablespoons pasta cooked water.
  20. Serve masala pasta hot.
  21. In a large pot, bring 6 cups water to a rolling boil. Add one teaspoon salt, a few drops of oil (optional) and pasta.
  22. Cook till al dente on a medium flame. I suggest referring the instructions on the pack of pasta for the correct timings. It takes 8 to 9 mins to cook penne.
  23. While the pasta cooks, rinse and chop the veggies.
  24. When the pasta is cooked to al dente, reserve ¾ cup pasta cooked water in a cup and drain the pasta off to a colander. Optionally if you want you may rinse them under running water.
  25. How to make Masala Pasta
  26. Heat oil & butter (optional) in a pan. When the oil turns hot, add cumin seeds & let them splutter.
  27. Stir in the garlic and ginger & saute for a minute.
  28. Add onions and sauté them on a medium high heat until they turn golden.
  29. Add chopped veggies and fry for about 2 to 3 minutes. Stir in the ground spices & salt.
  30. Time to add the tomatoes & the ketchup/ passata (optional). Mix well and cook on a medium heat until the tomatoes cook down and the raw flavor of tomatoes is gone. You will also see oil begins to separate from the masala & the veggies are cooked through. Else cover & cook on a low heat.
  31. Pour ¼ to ½ cup pasta cooked water or plain water. Mix well & cook for a few minutes, until the sauce turns thick & saucy. (Do not over cook)
  32. Stir in kasuri methi & cooked pasta. Taste test & adjust salt. Cover and cook on a medium heat for 2 to 3 mins, until the pasta turns hot & steaming.
  33. If you want you may stir in heavy cream or grated cheese.
  34. Garnish masala pasta with coriander leaves. Serve hot.

Notes

You may leave out the mixed veggies – carrot, bell peppers, peas & corn from the recipe and make the basic masala pasta. Tomato ketchup/ passata/ store bought bottled tomato puree/ tomato paste is used for a umami flavor. Adjust the quantity to suit your taste. You may add more when you stir in the pasta to the sauce. For a creamy taste, you can add 2 to 4 tablespoons heavy cream or cooking cream. Alternately blend 15 soaked cashews or 4 tablespoons of almond flour with 4 tablespoons of water in a small chutney grinder. You will need to add 2 to 4 tbsps more water & blend again until you get a smooth cream. Stir 2 to 3 tbsps of this after you switch off the stove. You can also make the masala ahead and refrigerate for 2 days. Heat up with a splash of water and add the cooked paste.
