Northwoods Roasted Potatoes

Servings: Serves: 8 · Prep: 5 minutes · Cook: 60-70 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 Cups chunked red potatoes
  • 1 Cup coarsely chopped yellow onion
  • 2 TB NORTHWOODS SEASONING
  • 2 TB olive oil

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°. Coarsely chop the onion and place in a large bowl. Leaving the skins on, cut potatoes into bite-sized pieces and add to the onions. Toss the potatoes and onions with the NORTHWOODS SEASONING until well coated. Then toss with the olive oil. Pour the potatoes into a 9x13 baking dish (or any other high-sided pan) and place in the oven. Stir the potatoes every 10 minutes for the first 30 minutes, then bake uninterrupted for an additional 30-40 minutes or until potatoes are nice and brown.
