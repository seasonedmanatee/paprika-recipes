Slow Cooker Chicken

Servings: 5 · Prep: 10 min · Cook: 3 hrs

Ingredients

  • 800 g Chicken breasts, skinless, about 5, sliced into cutlets
  • 360 ml Chicken Stock
  • 240 ml Double Cream, 48% fat. Lighter cream, including US heavy cream at about 36%, goes in at the end rather than the start.
  • 50 g Gourmet Sun-Dried Tomatoes, packed in oil, drained, and keep 1 tbsp of that oil for the paste in step 1
  • 1 tbsp Tomato Puree
  • 2 tbsp Plain Flour
  • 4 Garlic cloves, finely sliced
  • 1 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1 tsp Oregano
  • 1 tsp Sweet Hungarian Paprika, sweet rather than smoked or hot; Hungarian is the best of them, but any sweet paprika works
  • ½ tsp Chilli Powder, (optional)
  • Salt, (to taste)
  • 10 g Fresh Basil, chopped, for garnish
  • 25 g Parmesan Cheese, grated, for garnish
  • 10 Cherry tomatoes, for garnish
  • Pasta, to serve

Directions

  1. Prepare the Tomato-Flour Paste
  2. Drain the sun-dried tomatoes and keep 1 tbsp of the oil from the jar. In a small bowl, whisk the tomato puree, the flour and that oil into a smooth paste. Without the oil it stays thick and crumbly however long you work at it.
  3. Combine Ingredients in Slow Cooker
  4. Pour the chicken stock, black pepper, oregano, paprika and chilli powder into the slow cooker, and the double cream too if it is 48% fat. Lighter cream, US heavy cream included, waits until the end. Add the tomato-flour paste you prepared earlier and whisk everything together.
  5. Add Garlic and Sun-Dried Tomatoes
  6. Gently stir in the sliced garlic and diced sun-dried tomatoes.
  7. Add the Chicken
  8. Place the chicken breast cutlets into the slow cooker, ensuring they are fully submerged in the sauce.
  9. Optional: For added flavour, you can sear the chicken in a pan until golden brown before adding it to the slow cooker.
  10. Cover the slow cooker and set it to Low for 3-4 hours, or High for 1.5-2 hours if you are short of time. Low is the better setting: high heat can scorch a cream sauce, and breast meat goes tough if it is pushed. The chicken is done at 74C in the thickest part.
  11. Garnish
  12. If you held the cream back, stir it in now with the cooker off and let it sit under the lid for 10 minutes. Then garnish with grated Parmesan, chopped basil and sliced cherry tomatoes, and adjust the seasoning.
  13. Serve and Enjoy
  14. Plate the chicken over your choice of pasta or mashed potato. Pair with a glass of wine and share with someone special!
