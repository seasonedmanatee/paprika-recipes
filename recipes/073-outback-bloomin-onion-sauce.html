Outback Bloomin Onion Sauce

Servings: 4 · Prep: 10 min · Cook: 10 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 teaspoons ketchup
  • 2 tablespoons horseradish (little less)
  • 1/4 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/8 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 dash ground black pepper
  • 1 dash cayenne pepper

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients and mix well. Cover and place in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving.
