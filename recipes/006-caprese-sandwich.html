Caprese Sandwich

Servings: Servings: 4 sandwiches · Prep: 15 mins · Cook: 5 mins

Ingredients

  • 1X 2X 3X
  • 4-6 ciabatta rolls, see notes for details and alternatives
  • 1 pound fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • 1 ½ pounds fresh Campari or Roma tomatoes, cut into roughly ½ inch slices
  • 1 large handful fresh basil leaves
  • 1 large garlic clove, peeled
  • Good-quality thick balsamic vinegar
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Coarse finishing salt, to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to the broil setting. Slice the rolls lengthwise to create both top and bottom pieces.
  2. Broil.
  3. Broil the bread for a brief 2-4 minutes until it warms and just starts to toast.
  4. Flavor.
  5. Rub a peeled garlic clove on each piece of bread.
  6. Moisten.
  7. Drizzle each piece with olive oil, making sure to be generous without saturating.
  8. Layer.
  9. Place mozzarella slices over each bottom piece. Add tomato slices to completely cover the mozzarella. Layer basil leaves over the tomatoes.
  10. Drizzle.
  11. Drizzle the bottom halves with balsamic and sprinkle with salt.
  12. Serve.
  13. Complete the sandwiches by adding the top halves. Enjoy!
  14. Preheat oven to broil. Slice rolls lengthwise with a sharp knife to create tops and bottoms.
  15. Lay the tops and bottoms cut-side up on a baking sheet. Broil bread for 2-4 minutes until warm and just starting to toast.
  16. Remove from the oven and rub the peeled garlic clove on each piece of bread. You may not use the whole clove.
  17. Drizzle each piece will olive oil (generously but not soaking).
  18. Lay mozzarella slices across each bottom piece of bread, covering completely. Add tomato slices, completely covering the mozzarella. Add basil leaves, completely covering the tomato slices.
  19. Drizzle bottom halves with balsamic. Sprinkle with salt.
  20. Add top halves. Slice sandwiches in halve if desired. Enjoy!

Notes

Ciabatta is classic! The amounts listed above are specifically for 4 long, rectangular ciabatta roll sandwiches. If you can only find small square rolls, this recipe will yield about 6 sandwiches. If you use a full ciabatta loaf, it will yield about 4 large sandwiches. If you can’t find ciabatta, you can also use focaccia, a baguette, or really any other bread or rolls you desire. I like to buy pre-sliced fresh mozzarella to make life easier! You can slice your own mozzarella if preferred. You can also use buffalo mozzarella!
