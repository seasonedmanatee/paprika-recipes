Homemade Fettuccine Alfredo: Italian Cheese & Butter Noodles

Servings: 4 · Prep: 5 mins · Cook: 10 mins

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Unsalted Butter room temperature
  • 4 oz Parmigiano-Reggiano grated
  • Kosher Salt & Pepper
  • 1 lb Fresh Tagliatelle or Fettuccine Pasta or 350g dried

Directions

  1. In a large heatproof bowl, combine butter and cheese.
  2. In a medium pot of salted boiling water, cook pasta until al dente. Using tongs, a strainer, or a spider, transfer pasta to bowl with cheese and butter.
  3. Add 1/2 cup hot pasta-cooking water, then, using tongs, toss repeatedly until butter is melted and a creamy, emulsified sauce forms. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Sprinkle with additional parmesan cheese and serve immediately.
